**Long Description (900 chars — Capterra/G2/SoftwareSuggest):**
LedgerFlow is a Reconciliation OS built for Indian Chartered Accountants and finance teams.

Upload CSV/Excel/PDF from HDFC, ICICI, SBI, Axis, Kotak — LedgerFlow auto-matches by UTR, date and amount with 98%+ precision, flags duplicates, round-figures and out-of-order entries, and lets you review only exceptions.

Chase clients on WhatsApp in one tap, verify GST/PAN without leaving the app, and export Tally XML + Excel with vouchers and audit notes. Every firm is fully tenant-isolated (separate DB tenant_id, bcrypt + httpOnly sessions) — your books never mix. No demo data. 60-second setup, free plan (3 clients / 50 docs/mo), cancel anytime.

Perfect for CA firms closing 10–500 clients/month who are tired of VLOOKUP, copy-paste WhatsApp, and rebuilding Tally vouchers.
